01 / The idea

Not just code.
What it fixes.

Every build starts with a real annoyance: a group chat that can't pick a place, a family juggling care schedules, an event losing its venue, a merchant drowning in disputes. I find that friction, model it properly, and ship something people can actually use.

  1. 01

    The problem

    Start from what people actually struggle with, not from the tech.

  2. 02

    The model

    Get the data right: graphs, schedules, constraints, money flows.

  3. 03

    The build

    Full stack, end to end. Vue on the front, Python or JS behind it.

  4. 04

    The proof

    Tests and checks before demos: 55 automated tests, 35 validation checks.
